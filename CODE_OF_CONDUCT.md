# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the `agent-env-doctor` community a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of differing technical conclusions and evidence.
- Give and accept constructive feedback in good faith.
- Focus discussion on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy. Redact sensitive task data, logs, and credentials before sharing them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, tokens, or sensitive logs in public project channels.
- Sustained disruption of technical discussion or project work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Responses may include a warning, temporary restriction from project spaces, or removal from project spaces.

At the time this policy was added, no private reporting channel has been configured. Do not put sensitive information in a public issue or pull request. Once the project is published on GitHub, maintainers should configure a private reporting channel for conduct concerns and update this policy with it.
